6. Safety Considerations

All maintenance activities must follow strict safety protocols. The pressure sensor at the wellhead is critical for preventing overpressure conditions. Temperature monitoring at the separator prevents thermal runaway. Level sensors at storage prevent overflow incidents. Emergency shutdown procedures must be tested quarterly.

Critical Safety Points:
• Never bypass pressure relief valves
• Maintain sensor calibration schedules
• Test emergency shutdown systems quarterly
• Keep fire suppression systems operational
• Ensure all personnel wear appropriate PPE
