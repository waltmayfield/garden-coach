Safety Procedures for Industrial Operations

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
   - Hard hats must be worn at all times in designated areas
   - Safety glasses are required when working with machinery
   - Steel-toed boots are mandatory on the production floor
   - Hearing protection must be used in high-noise areas

2. Emergency Procedures
   - In case of fire, activate the nearest fire alarm
   - Evacuate using the designated emergency exits
   - Assemble at the designated muster point
   - Do not re-enter the building until cleared by safety personnel

3. Equipment Operation
   - Only trained and authorized personnel may operate machinery
   - Perform pre-operation safety checks before starting equipment
   - Report any equipment malfunctions immediately
   - Follow lockout/tagout procedures during maintenance

4. Hazardous Materials
   - Consult Safety Data Sheets (SDS) before handling chemicals
   - Use appropriate PPE when working with hazardous materials
   - Store chemicals in designated areas with proper labeling
   - Report spills immediately to the safety coordinator

5. Incident Reporting
   - Report all incidents, near-misses, and unsafe conditions
   - Complete incident reports within 24 hours
   - Cooperate with safety investigations
   - Participate in corrective action implementation

This document is part of the company's safety management system and should be reviewed regularly by all personnel.
