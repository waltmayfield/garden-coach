

Vision:
- The world would be a better place if more people gardened. They would have a closer connection to nature, healthier minds and bodies, better food, richer comunities, and cities would be prettier.
- An app to guide beginner gardeners on a successful first season would enable asperational gardeners to build a gardening habit.
- Like strava but for gardening

Pain Point:
- Gardening is hard. People often have a desire to garden, but don't know where to get started. This leaves them frusturated, and they often abandon gardening, or labor through the first season, only to get a tiny harvest, and then quit.

Things that have to go right for a person to build a gardening habit:
1. The time to garden
2. The space to garden
3. The knowledge to know how to garden
4. The ability to procure required gardening supplies, and transport them to the garden.

Target features:
1. Natural language -> garden task list with steps and visuals of what to expect.
1. Chat interface to
    - Troubleshoot the garden
    - Update the goal / task list
1. Harvest yield prediction calendar based on planting plan
1. System to organize trading of crops based on planting plan
    - Distributed comunity agriculture
1. Social aspect to post about harvests, tips, ...
